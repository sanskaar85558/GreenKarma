The Solution

Meet GreenKarma: AI Reward Engine

An innovative eco-tech framework that directly pays citizens for sorting domestic waste at home, converting ecological duty into real, spendable utility tokens.

Frictionless Capture

No complex smart-bins required. Fast smartphone photos are all it takes to trigger the verification funnel.

AI Segregation Verification

Tangible Compensation

Social Impact

Empowering India's Informal Force

Over 4 million informal waste pickers sustain India's circular economy but lack structural financial inclusion or legal identification.

  • Formal Digital Profiles: Registered as Verified Collectors on the platform, establishing clean work profiles.
  • Uber-Style Pickup Alerts: Get immediate geolocation alerts of verified pre-segregated waste logs nearby.
  • Verifiable Ledger Records: Creating transaction histories allowing integration into banking and health schemes.
Economics

A Self-Sustaining Revenue Model

The network rewards are funded via municipal operational savings, corporate ecological mandates, and international carbon trades.

Municipal Logistics

Pre-segregated waste reduces sorting labor costs. Cities route up to 40% of operational budgets to cover reward pools.

EPR Credit Clearance

FMCG brands acquire verified high-purity recycling offset ledger entries to satisfy Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) regulations.

Carbon Mitigation

Diverting biodegradable matter avoids methane release. These reductions are aggregated and sold globally.
